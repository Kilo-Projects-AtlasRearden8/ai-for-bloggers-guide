07 Scheduling

You do not need to publish every day. A simple, repeatable schedule works better than an ambitious one you cannot keep.

Step 1: Pick a frequency

Choose one:

  • Once a week
  • Twice a month
  • Once a month

Start small. You can publish more often later.

Step 2: Pick a day and time

Choose a specific day and time, such as "Mondays at 9 a.m." Put it on your calendar or planner.

Step 3: Use your platform's scheduler

Most free blog platforms let you write a post now and set it to publish later:

  • Blogger: Schedule post → set date and time
  • WordPress.com: Publish → schedule
  • Substack: Schedule send

Write your post ahead of time, set the schedule, and you are done.

Step 4: Build a buffer

Try to have 2–3 posts written and scheduled before you go public. That way, if life gets busy, your blog keeps running.

Simple monthly plan

WeekTask
Week 1Draft post, add image, schedule it
Week 2Draft post, add image, schedule it
Week 3Draft post, add image, schedule it
Week 4Rest, or repurpose an old post (see 08 Repurposing)

Quick check

  • I picked a publishing frequency
  • I picked a day and time
  • I scheduled at least one post ahead of time
