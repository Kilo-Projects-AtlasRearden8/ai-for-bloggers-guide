06 Images

Images make a blog post easier to read. You do not need to take photos or buy anything.

Step 1: Use free image sources

Good free options (no attribution required for most uses):

SiteBest forLink
UnsplashHigh-quality photosunsplash.com
PexelsPhotos + videospexels.com
PixabayIllustrations + vectorspixabay.com
Wikimedia CommonsEducational/historicalcommons.wikimedia.org

Search for your topic, download an image, and upload it to your blog.

Tip: Search for "minimal" or "flat lay" + your topic for clean, blog-friendly images.

Step 2: Make images with free AI

Many free AI tools can generate images from a description.

Free tools to try:

  • Bing Image Creator (free with Microsoft account) — powered by DALL-E
  • Leonardo.ai (150 free credits/day) — good for illustrations
  • Adobe Firefly (free tier) — good for text effects
  • Local via Stable Diffusion (if you have a GPU) — 100% private

Prompt formulas that work well for blogs:

"A simple, friendly illustration of [topic]. Clean flat design, bright pastel colors, minimal details, no text, white background."
"Flat lay photo of [topic items] on a light wood desk. Natural lighting, minimal composition, high quality, no people."
"Minimal line art icon of [topic]. Single weight black lines, white background, simple and clear."

Example for a budgeting post:

"Flat lay of a simple spreadsheet on a laptop screen, a coffee mug, and a notebook with a pen. Clean minimal style, natural morning light, light wood desk, no people."

Download the result and upload it to your blog.

Step 3: Add alt text

Alt text describes the image for people who cannot see it. Write one short sentence:

  • "A person typing on a laptop at a desk."
  • "A hand holding a smartphone with a blog open."
  • "A simple spreadsheet showing income and expenses."

Most blog platforms ask for alt text when you upload an image. Fill it in — it helps with accessibility and SEO.

Step 4: Keep it small

Large images make pages slow to load. If an image is bigger than you need, resize it before uploading.

Quick resize targets:

  • Blog header/featured image: 1200px wide max
  • In-post images: 800px wide max
  • File size: under 200 KB each if possible

Free tools to resize:

  • Mac: Preview → Tools → Adjust Size
  • Windows: Photos app → Resize
  • Online: squoosh.app (drag, drop, download)
  • Command line: magick input.jpg -resize 1200x output.jpg (ImageMagick)
Privacy reminder: do not upload photos that show real people, addresses, license plates, or private documents. Use stock or AI-generated images instead.

Quick check

  • I have at least one image in my post
  • The image has alt text
  • The image does not show private information
  • The image is under 200 KB (or reasonably sized)
