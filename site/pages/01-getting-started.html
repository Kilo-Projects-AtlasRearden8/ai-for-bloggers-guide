01 Getting Started

You do not need to spend money to start a blog with AI. Everything in this manual uses free tools.

Step 1: Pick a place to publish

Good free options include:

PlatformBest forYour URL will be
BloggerSimple posts, Google integrationyourname.blogspot.com
WordPress.comMore design control, pluginsyourname.wordpress.com
SubstackNewsletter + blog combinedyourname.substack.com
GitHub PagesLearning web basics, full controlyourname.github.io

Recommendation for total beginners: Blogger or Substack. Both take 5 minutes to set up and require zero technical knowledge.

Pick one and create your account. Use a name you are comfortable sharing publicly. Your blog is public, so treat everything you publish as public.

Step 2: Choose free AI tools

You do not need a paid AI subscription. Good free options:

Free chat AI (pick one):

  • Claude.ai (free tier, generous daily limits)
  • ChatGPT (free tier at chat.openai.com)
  • Gemini (free at gemini.google.com)
  • Local models via Ollama or LM Studio — runs on your computer, 100% private

Free image generation:

  • Bing Image Creator (free with Microsoft account)
  • Leonardo.ai (daily free credits)
  • Local via Stable Diffusion (if you have a decent GPU)

When you sign up, use an email address you do not mind sharing. Do not use your work email unless you are sure it is allowed.

Step 3: Learn the privacy rule before you write anything.
Never paste personal information into an AI tool. That means no names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, patient or client details, passwords, or account numbers — yours or anyone else's. Assume anything you type into a free AI tool could be seen by the company that runs it.

Step 4: Write your first post

Once your blog is set up and you know the privacy rule, move to 02 Topic Ideas to decide what to write about.

Quick check before you continue

  • I have a blog account on a free platform
  • I know which free AI tool I will use
  • I understand the privacy rule: never paste personal info
