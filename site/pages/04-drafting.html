04 Drafting

You write the post. The AI helps. You stay in charge.

Step 1: Draft one section at a time

Open your outline. Take the first section and ask the AI:

"I am writing a blog post for beginners. Write section [number]: [one-sentence goal from your outline]. Use plain English, short sentences, and a friendly tone. Do not use jargon. If you use a technical word, explain it in one sentence."

Write the AI's answer in your own words. Change anything that does not sound like you.

Step 2: Read it out loud

Read your draft aloud. If you stumble over a sentence, simplify it. If a sentence sounds like a robot, rewrite it in your own words.

Step 3: Check the privacy rule

Read the whole draft and ask:

  • Did I name any real people?
  • Did I share addresses, phone numbers, or emails?
  • Did I share patient, client, or customer details?
  • Did I paste any passwords or account numbers?

If yes, delete or change it before publishing. Read 09 Privacy.

Step 4: Add a short introduction and conclusion

Introduction: tell the reader what this post is about and why it matters. Keep it to 2–4 sentences.
Conclusion: tell the reader one small thing they can try today. End there.

Example drafting prompt

"Write a 300-word beginner section about choosing a blog name. Use plain English, short sentences, and a warm tone. Do not include personal information. Do not give medical advice."

Quick check

  • I drafted each section separately
  • I read the draft out loud
  • I checked for personal information
  • I added an introduction and conclusion
