# 03 Outlines

An outline keeps your post from wandering. It also makes writing faster because you always know what comes next.

## Step 1: Ask the AI for an outline

Tell the AI your topic and your reader. Try a prompt like this:

> "I am writing a blog post called '[your title]' for total beginners. Create a simple outline with an introduction, 4 short sections, and a conclusion. Use plain English. Keep each section to one sentence describing what it covers."

Paste your topic in place of `[your title]`.

## Step 2: Trim and reorder

Read the outline. Delete anything that does not help your reader. Move sections around until the order feels natural. A good beginner outline usually looks like:

1. Introduction — what this post is about and why it matters
2. Section 1 — the first thing the reader needs to know
3. Section 2 — the next step
4. Section 3 — a common mistake to avoid
5. Section 4 — a simple example or walkthrough
6. Conclusion — one thing the reader can try today

## Step 3: Fill in one-sentence goals

Under each section heading, write one sentence saying what that section will cover. This becomes your checklist while drafting.

## Example outline

**Title:** How to Start a Free Blog in 30 Minutes

1. Introduction — why start a blog, and why it does not have to cost money
2. Pick a free platform — Blogger, WordPress.com, or Substack
3. Create your account — email, password, blog name
4. Write your first post — title, body, publish button
5. Common mistake to avoid — do not share personal info in your posts
6. Conclusion — publish one post per week and keep going

## Quick check

- [ ] I have an outline for my post
- [ ] Each section has a one-sentence goal
- [ ] The order makes sense for a beginner reader
