# 09 Privacy

This is the most important rule in this manual:

## Never paste personal information into an AI tool.

That includes:

- Your full name, home address, phone number, or email address
- Anyone else's name, address, phone number, or email address
- Patient, client, customer, or coworker details
- Passwords, account numbers, credit card numbers
- Anything you would not want a stranger to read online

## Why this matters

Free AI tools are run by companies. Assume anything you type can be stored, reviewed, or used to improve their models. Once information is in an AI tool, you cannot easily take it back.

## How to stay safe

- Write your posts in general terms.
- Use made-up examples instead of real ones.
- Change names and details in stories you tell.
- Never paste a draft that contains private information into an AI just to "check the grammar."
- If you are a healthcare worker, do not share patient details — not even "a patient I had last week."

## If you make a mistake

If you paste something private by accident, do not panic. Stop using that chat, delete the conversation if the tool lets you, and do not put that information into any AI tool again. If it was someone else's private information, consider telling them so they can protect themselves.
