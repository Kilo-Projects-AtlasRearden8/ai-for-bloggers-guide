# 02 Topic Ideas

The hardest part of blogging is often deciding what to write about. Free AI can help you brainstorm.

## Step 1: Start with what you know

Make a quick list of:

- Things people ask you about
- Topics you enjoy talking about
- Problems you have solved recently
- Things you wish you had known a year ago

You do not need to be the world's leading expert. You only need to be one step ahead of your reader.

## Step 2: Ask a free AI for ideas

Open a free AI chat tool and try a prompt like this:

> "I want to start a blog for beginners. I know a little about [your topic]. Give me 10 simple blog post ideas that a total beginner would find useful. Keep the titles plain and practical."

Replace `[your topic]` with something real, like gardening, nursing, budgeting, chess, or video games.

If the ideas feel too advanced, ask again:

> "Make these simpler. Assume the reader has never done this before."

## Step 3: Pick one idea

Choose one idea that:

- Interests you
- Feels useful to someone else
- Does not require sharing private information (remember the privacy rule)

Write the idea down. That is your topic for this post.

## Example prompt collection

Copy and paste these, swapping in your own topic:

- "What are the most common questions beginners ask about [topic]?"
- "Give me 5 blog post ideas for someone brand new to [topic]."
- "What mistakes do beginners make with [topic], and how can they avoid them?"

## Privacy reminder

Do not paste real patient names, customer details, or your personal information into the AI. Keep your examples general.

- [ ] I picked one topic for my first post
- [ ] My topic does not require sharing private information
