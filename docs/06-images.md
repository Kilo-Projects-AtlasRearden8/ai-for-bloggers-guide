# 06 Images

Images make a blog post easier to read. You do not need to take photos or buy anything.

## Step 1: Use free image sources

Good free options:

- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) — free photos, free to use
- **Pexels** (pexels.com) — free photos and videos
- **Pixabay** (pixabay.com) — free photos and illustrations

Search for your topic, download an image, and upload it to your blog.

## Step 2: Make images with free AI

Many free AI tools can generate images from a description. Try a prompt like this:

> "A simple, friendly illustration of someone writing at a laptop. Bright colors, clean style, no text."

Download the result and upload it to your blog.

## Step 3: Add alt text

Alt text describes the image for people who cannot see it. Write one short sentence:

- "A person typing on a laptop at a desk."
- "A hand holding a smartphone with a blog open."

Most blog platforms ask for alt text when you upload an image. Fill it in.

## Step 4: Keep it small

Large images make pages slow to load. If an image is bigger than you need, resize it before uploading. Many computers have a free "Preview" or "Photos" app that can resize images.

## Privacy reminder

Do not upload photos that show real people, addresses, license plates, or private documents. Use stock or AI-generated images instead.
