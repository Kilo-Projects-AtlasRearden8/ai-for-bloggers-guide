## How to use it

1. Print this page.
2. At the start of the month, write your goal and your publish day.
3. For each week, write your topic and headline, then check off outline, draft, image, and scheduled as you finish them.
4. Use the "Ideas to try later" list for topics from [02 Topic Ideas](02-topic-ideas.md).

## Privacy reminder

Keep this planner on paper or in a private file. Do not paste your planner contents into an AI tool if they contain names or private details.
