# 05 Headlines

A headline is the first thing people see. It should tell them what the post is about and why to read it. You do not need clickbait.

## Step 1: Be clear first

Good beginner headlines tell the reader exactly what they will get:

- "How to Start a Free Blog in 30 Minutes"
- "5 Simple Ways to Save Money on Groceries"
- "A Beginner's Guide to Chess Openings"

Avoid:

- "You Won't Believe What Happened Next"
- "This One Weird Trick"

## Step 2: Ask the AI for options

Paste your draft (without personal information) into the AI and ask:

> "Give me 5 plain-English headlines for this blog post. Keep them simple, honest, and useful. No clickbait."

Pick the one that feels most helpful.

## Step 3: Test it

Read your headline alone. Does it tell you what the post is about? Would you click it if you were a beginner? If not, rewrite it.

## Example headlines for one post

Topic: starting a free blog

- "How to Start a Blog for Free (No Experience Needed)"
- "Free Blogging Platforms for Beginners"
- "Write Your First Blog Post in 5 Easy Steps"

## Quick check

- [ ] My headline says what the post is about
- [ ] My headline uses plain English
- [ ] My headline is honest (no exaggeration)
