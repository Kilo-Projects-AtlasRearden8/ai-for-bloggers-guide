# 01 Getting Started

You do not need to spend money to start a blog with AI. Everything in this manual uses free tools.

## Step 1: Pick a place to publish

Good free options include:

- **Blogger** — free hosting, simple, owned by Google
- **WordPress.com** — free plan with a wordpress.com address
- **Substack** — free to start, good for newsletter-style blogs
- **GitHub Pages** — free static hosting (best if you want to learn a little tech)

Pick one and create your account. Use a name you are comfortable sharing publicly. Your blog is public, so treat everything you publish as public.

## Step 2: Choose free AI tools

You do not need a paid AI subscription. Good free options:

- Free chat-based AI tools from major providers (many have generous free daily limits)
- Free local AI models that run on your own computer
- Free image generators with daily credit limits

When you sign up, use an email address you do not mind sharing. Do not use your work email unless you are sure it is allowed.

## Step 3: Learn the privacy rule before you write anything

**Privacy rule: never paste personal information into an AI tool.**

That means no:

- Your full name, address, phone number, or email
- Someone else's name, address, phone number, or email
- Patient, client, or customer details
- Passwords, account numbers, or financial information
- Anything you would not want a stranger to read

Assume anything you type into a free AI tool could be seen by the company that runs it. Keep your posts general and helpful without naming real people or sharing private details.

See [09 Privacy](09-privacy.md) for more.

## Step 4: Write your first post

Once your blog is set up and you know the privacy rule, move to [02 Topic Ideas](02-topic-ideas.md) to decide what to write about.

- [ ] I have a blog account on a free platform
- [ ] I know which free AI tool I will use
- [ ] I understand the privacy rule: never paste personal info
